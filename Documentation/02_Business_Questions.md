# Business Questions

The following business questions will guide the analysis of the banking datasets.

## BQ1. Customer & Card Adoption

Which customer segments do not have bank cards, and what opportunities exist to increase card adoption?

## BQ2. Loan Portfolio Analysis

Which customer segments take loans, what are their repayment patterns, and where are the major loan risks?

## BQ3. Transaction Behaviour

How do transaction frequency, total transaction value, and average transaction value vary across customers?

## BQ4. Credit & Debit Transaction Analysis

What are the daily and monthly credit, debit, and net transaction trends?

## BQ5. Regional Analysis

How do customer activity, card adoption, loan usage, and transaction patterns vary across counties?

## BQ6. Customer Segmentation

How can customers be grouped based on transaction behaviour and banking product usage to identify suitable business opportunities?

## BQ7. Marketing Opportunities

How can customer behaviour and regional patterns help the bank identify suitable target segments for product promotions?
